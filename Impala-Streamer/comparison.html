The honest pitch

This isn't a rebellion. It's a second option.

Plex is a mature, capable product with a long track record, and plenty of people are happy paying for it. Impala Streamer isn't trying to out-feature it or win a popularity contest — it's built for the smaller group of people who would rather own the box than rent the experience.

If you already hold a lifetime license, keep it. This comparison exists so that decision is informed, not so it gets reversed.

What you getA self-hosted media platform built for people who want direct control over their own library.

Where Plex still wins

No pitch is honest without the tradeoffs.

More apps, more polish

Years of native app investment across TVs, phones, and consoles that a smaller project hasn't matched yet.

Bigger ecosystem

Plugins, metadata agents, and community tooling with a much longer track record.

Less networking work

Plex's hosted relay and discovery remove some of the setup that self-hosting otherwise requires.

More hands on deck

A larger team means faster answers to obscure edge cases than a single-developer project can offer today.

Try it before you decide

Keep Plex. Just also take a look at Impala.

No pressure to switch, no migration required to try it. See whether owning the box changes how you feel about renting the experience.
