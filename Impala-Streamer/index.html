Why now

Corporate marketing, invasive advertising, and data exploitation have intruded far enough into personal space. Impala Streamer is a practical step toward reclaiming privacy, ownership, and control over personal media.

01 / The product thesis

A product layer for libraries people have already cultivated and invested time in.

Impala Streamer provides the experience between a personal media collection and the people trusted to use it. The core value is not more content; it is better control, discovery, reliability, and ownership.

01

Organize

Bring personal audio and video into a coherent library with playlists, search, artwork, and clear states.

02

Experience

Make playback feel polished across modern HTML5-capable devices, from a focused music player to full video sessions.

03

Share intentionally

Support invited access and private collaboration without turning a personal library into a public platform.

04

Ownership

Impala Streamer is yours to operate. There is no Impala subscription, behavioral tracking, advertising profile, or recommendation algorithm. Optional metadata enrichment can provide album artwork, movie posters, and descriptive information. If anonymity matters more than that enrichment, leave External Metadata & Artwork off. When enabled, limited media search terms and ordinary network information are processed by Netlify and public metadata providers; Impala identities, credentials, playlists, and file paths are not sent.

02 / The opportunity

Practical value for users. Durable value for the business.

I built Impala Streamer after reaching the limit of how far I would allow marketing to insert itself into my life. Music was one space that still felt solely mine. When promotional pages began appearing between a service's login screen and my own media library, that was enough.
