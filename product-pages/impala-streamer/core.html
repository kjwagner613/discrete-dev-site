Impala Streamer Core

The local-first audio and video player. Core runs on your machine and remains free forever.

Your library. Your machine.

  • Local Library: internal storage or USB-attached volumes.
  • Full Local Indexing: fast, private, and offline.
  • Local Media Server: playback on local-network devices with an HTML5-compatible browser.
  • Formats: MP3, FLAC, MP4, and MKV with automatic transcoding where needed.
