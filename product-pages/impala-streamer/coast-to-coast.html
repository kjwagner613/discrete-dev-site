Coast to Coast

It gets more and more challenging to create the space to sit with someone, and simply enjoy watching a show or movie. Storytelling goes far back in our history, and it can be soothing, in an otherwise demanding world. Coast to Coast was built to bridge the physical gap of distance. Enjoy a shared experience, and with your attention on the screen, the open communication can make them feel they are somehow in the room with you.

One less obstacle to being together

Coast to Coast uses SyncPlay to synchronize media playback across different locations, ensuring both participants are at the sane part of the story. With the addition of vocal communication, either hands free or push to talk, it's the next best thing to being there.
