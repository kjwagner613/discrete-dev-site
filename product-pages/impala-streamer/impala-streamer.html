What Impala Streamer Is

Impala is built on a simple premise: technology should respect attention. It should behave predictably, remain faithful to its stated purpose, and avoid manipulative patterns. Impala is not a feed, a funnel, or a growth mechanism. It is a listening tool.

  • No algorithmic recommendations
  • No engagement traps
  • No ads, no tracking, no harvesting
  • Local-first, user-controlled playback
  • Designed for reliability, not addiction

Design Principles

Impala is governed by a short set of design principles that guide the interface, storage model, playback behavior, and artist-facing tools.

  • Simplicity is reliability. Remove complexity, remove failure.
  • Stay faithful to purpose. A player should play, clearly and consistently.
  • No iteration in the body of code. Stability over novelty.
  • Sovereign systems. The user owns the experience.
  • Zero manipulation. No algorithms shaping behavior.

User Edition

The User Edition is the everyday listening environment: simple, calm, and predictable.

  • Local playlists with native manipulation to combine, reorder, and manage tracks
  • Starred track tag system allowing dynamic content grouping.
  • Minimal UI
  • Local or Cloud Library
  • Live Stream (webcast) with a catalog of reliable streams, and an open field for custom user input.
  • Direct control over playback
  • SyncPlay, where a group can watch and listen together and transport control is either shared across the group, or one master controller.
  • Coast to Coast, where two people can watch a video together, with SyncPlay function and an open mic, or push to talk, adding conversation to the experience.
  • Impala's core handles all shared, sovereign functionality, while additional services remain fully modular and optional. These services operate in discrete processing environments and never affect core performance.

Artist Edition

The Artist Edition, currently in development, is a formalized environment for musicians to manage creative assets, control distribution, and maintain sovereignty over their work. It is built on the same principles as the User Edition, but with expanded capabilities.

  • Asset management (audio, metadata, artwork)
  • Rights and compliance visibility
  • Audience communication tools
  • Playlist and message-link controls
  • Temporary local storage for drafts and experiments

Ephemeral and Sovereign

The core rule of Impala:

Impala stores nothing itself.

All data is maintained in browser cache and is cleared on exit. The user owns the experience.


This principle ensures that Impala never becomes a data-harvesting platform. User activity stays local, temporary, and under direct user control.

Why Impala Matters

Sovereignty has drifted quietly for decades, replaced piece by piece by algorithms, funnels, and behavioral shaping. Impala is the beginning of bringing it back — starting with listening, and extending outward into every domain where calm, user-owned systems should exist.

  • Reduces exposure to manipulative algorithms
  • Eliminates advertising pressure
  • Supports mental well-being by minimizing distractions
  • Encourages mindful listening experiences

Optimizing for Humanity

Optimizing for Humans means designing technology that respects attention, behaves predictably, absorbs friction wherever possible, protects user agency, and stays faithful to purpose. Impala is built on this principle — a calm, sovereign listening environment created for people, not algorithms, not advertising, and not for harvesting attention.
