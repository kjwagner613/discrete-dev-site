Impala Artist Edition — Formal Specifications

1. System Identity

System Type: Local, sovereign media viewer Core Principle: Source → Experience

Non-Functions:

  • No storage
  • No retention
  • No distribution
  • No commerce
  • No accounts
  • No analytics
  • No cloud dependencies
  • Impala is not a platform, marketplace, publisher, distributor, or merchant.

2. Artist Edition Overview

The Artist Edition is a themed, isolated environment allowing musicians to:
  • Load their own music locally
  • Curate playlists
  • Broadcast messages/links
  • Temporarily expose tracks to the collective catalog
  • Maintain full control over their catalog and revenue
  • Impala never stores or distributes their music.

3. Artist Bucket Specification

3.1 Definition

A local, isolated, temporary container holding the artist’s assets.

3.2 Contents

  • Audio tracks
  • Visuals
  • Playlist definitions
  • Edition identity metadata

3.3 Requirements

  • Bucket must remain private
  • Bucket must never expose raw URLs
  • Bucket must never be downloadable
  • Bucket must be fully removable
  • Bucket must not persist after removal
  • Bucket must not sync to cloud

3.4 Lifecycle

  • Created when artist loads assets
  • Active during playback
  • Destroyed when artist retracts access
  • Leaves no residual data

4. Player Identifier Specification

4.1 Purpose

Defines the identity of the Artist Edition.

4.2 Fields

  • artistName
  • editionName
  • themeColor (optional)
  • badge (optional)

4.3 Behavior

  • Displayed in UI
  • Stored locally
  • No cloud sync
  • No accounts required

5. Control Switch Specification

A critical safety mechanism ensuring artist control.

5.1 Modes

A. Keep Local

  • Tracks remain inside the artist bucket only
  • No exposure to collective catalog
  • No risk to artist's commercial revenue
  • No distribution or leakage

B. Add to Collective

  • Tracks appear in shared catalog
  • Exposure is temporary
  • Artist can retract at any time
  • No downloads
  • No file access
  • No persistence

5.2 Requirements

  • Must clearly indicate current mode
  • Must allow switching between modes
  • Must enforce catalog updates
  • Must prevent leakage of local tracks
  • Must be reversible
  • Must update catalog immediately
  • Must not leave cached copies

6. Message/Link Pipe Specification

6.1 Purpose

A one-way broadcast channel for artists.

6.2 Capabilities

Artist can send:
  • Text messages
  • Announcements
  • Playlist updates
  • External links (CD sales, Bandcamp, merch, tour dates, etc.)

6.3 Restrictions

  • Not a chat system
  • Not a social feed
  • Not a platform
  • No inbound messages
  • No analytics
  • No tracking

7. Playlist Specification

7.1 Artist Capabilities

  • Create playlists
  • Reorder tracks
  • Select visuals
  • Select transitions
  • Define vibe/theme

7.2 Fan Capabilities

  • View only
  • No editing
  • No interaction layer

8. Temporary Local Storage Specification

8.1 Definition

Music is stored only on the artist’s own device or environment

8.2 Implications

  • Artist retains full ownership
  • Artist retains full responsibility
  • Discrete Development, LLC has zero liability
  • Discrete Development, LLC does not store, retain, or distribute anything

8.3 Requirements

  • No caching beyond runtime
  • No persistence after removal
  • No replication
  • No cloud sync

9. Liability & Compliance Boundary

9.1 Discrete Development, LLC Is Not

  • A host
  • A distributor
  • A publisher
  • A merchant
  • A payment processor
  • A rights holder
  • A storage provider

9.2 Discrete Development, LLC Does Not

  • Store music
  • Retain music
  • Distribute music
  • Sell music
  • Process payments
  • Take a cut
  • Track users

9.3 Artist Responsibilities

  • Their storage
  • Their catalog
  • Their revenue
  • Their taxes
  • Their compliance
